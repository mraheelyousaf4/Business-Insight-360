## 4. Supply chain

**Question:** How accurate is the demand forecast, and where is the stock risk?

**Period:** FY2020 against last year. Source: [`06_supply_chain.jpg`](../images/06_supply_chain.jpg)

| KPI | FY2020 | Last year |
|---|---:|---:|
| Forecast accuracy | 72.99% | 86.45% |
| Net error | 492K | 0.64M |
| ABS error | 6M | 1.55M |

**Monthly forecast accuracy:** stayed between 78.78% and 82.44% from September 2019 to February 2020, fell to 15.40% in March 2020, then recovered to 81.33% by June 2020.

| Segment | FCA % | FCA % LY | Net error | Risk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Storage | 81.01% | 80.25% | 698K | EI |
| Notebook | 76.65% | 83.02% | 147K | EI |
| Peripherals | 75.18% | 85.06% | 193K | EI |
| Accessories | 71.42% | 90.20% | -168K | OOS |
| Desktop | 70.07% | (blank) | 0K | OOS |
| Networking | 52.50% | 81.50% | -379K | OOS |

**Answer:** Forecast accuracy dropped from 86.45% to 72.99%, driven by a sharp fall in March 2020. Networking was the least accurate segment at 52.50%. Storage, Notebook and Peripherals were over-forecast (excess inventory risk), while Accessories and Networking were under-forecast (out of stock risk).

EI = excess inventory (forecast above actual sales). OOS = out of stock (forecast below actual sales).
